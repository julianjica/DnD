The game data in `lib/srd/data/` is derived from the **System Reference Document 5.1**.

The machine-readable form was obtained from the 5e-bits **5e-database** project
(https://github.com/5e-bits/5e-database, `src/2014/en`) and converted by
`scripts/srd/import.ts`. Changes made: fields were renamed, restructured and trimmed;
monster actions and spells were parsed into structured mechanics; proficiency ids were
normalized.

The "Criminal", "Sage" and "Soldier" backgrounds are adapted from the System Reference
Document 5.2 ("SRD 5.2") by Wizards of the Coast LLC, also licensed under CC-BY-4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode), with 2014-style mechanics and
short original descriptions.
